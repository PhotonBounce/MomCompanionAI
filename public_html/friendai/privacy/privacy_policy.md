# Friendai Privacy Policy

_Last updated: May 28, 2026_

Friendai is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Microphone Access:** Used only for speech recognition. No audio is stored or shared.
- **No Personal Data Storage:** Conversations are not stored on the device or sent to third parties, except the AI backend you configure.
- **Network Access:** Used to connect to your configured AI backend. No data is sent elsewhere.
- **Phone State & Calls:** Used only for emergency call features, if enabled by you.

## 2. How We Use Information
- To provide voice interaction and accessibility features.
- To enable emergency calling if you configure a caregiver contact.
- To improve app performance and user experience.

## 3. Data Sharing
- **We do not share your data** with third parties, advertisers, or analytics providers.
- Data is only sent to the AI backend you configure in the app settings.

## 4. Permissions
- **Microphone:** For speech recognition only.
- **Internet:** For connecting to the AI backend.
- **Phone:** For emergency call features (optional).
- **Storage:** For saving screenshots (optional, for QA/testing only).

## 5. Security
- All network communication uses secure protocols where possible.
- No sensitive data is stored on the device.

## 6. Children's Privacy
- Friendai is not intended for children under 13. No data is knowingly collected from children.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
- We may update this policy. Changes will be posted in the app and at the privacy policy URL.
