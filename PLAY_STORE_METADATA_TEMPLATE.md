## Title
Friendai

## Short Description
Your always-listening AI friend for seniors and caregivers. Safe, accessible, and private.

## Full Description
Friendai is a voice-activated assistant designed for seniors and caregivers. It provides conversation, reminders, and help, with privacy and accessibility as top priorities. Features include:
- Simple talk button for voice interaction
- Onboarding for caregivers to set up PIN and contacts
- Closed captioning for all speech
- Device check and troubleshooting
- No ads, no data selling, privacy by design

Caregivers can configure backend AI, PIN, and emergency contacts. The app works offline for basic features and never stores conversations.
